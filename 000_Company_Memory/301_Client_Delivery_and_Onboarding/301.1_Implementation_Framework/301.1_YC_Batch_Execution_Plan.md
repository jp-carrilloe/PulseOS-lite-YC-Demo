# YC Batch Execution Plan (The 11-Week Sprint)

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-04
**Author/Editor:** @ARK
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose
The definitive execution plan for the 11-week Y Combinator batch. This framework ensures every founder moves from "Accepted" to "Demo Day Ready" with maximum velocity and product-market fit.

---

## Phase 1: Onboarding & Infrastructure (Week 0)
- **SAFE Signing:** Execution of the $500k standard deal.
- **Bookface Activation:** Granting access to the internal network, perks, and founder directory.
- **Batch Kickoff:** Orientation dinner and introduction to the "YC Way."
- **The Metric that Matters:** Founders select their primary growth metric (usually revenue or active users).

## Phase 2: The Core Batch (Weeks 1-10)

### Weekly Cadence
- **Tuesday Dinners:** Guest speakers and community building.
- **Group Office Hours:** 4-6 companies meeting with 1-2 partners for peer feedback and goal tracking.
- **1:1 Office Hours:** Deep-dives into specific strategic or technical blockers.

### Key Milestones
- **Week 3: Baseline Check.** Is the product live? Are users talking back?
- **Week 6: Mid-Batch Retreat.** Recalibration of goals and peer bonding.
- **Week 8: Demo Day Prep Starts.** Drafting the pitch and designing the deck.
- **Week 10: Pitch Practice.** Intense feedback on the Demo Day presentation.

## Phase 3: Distribution & Demo Day (Week 11)
- **Demo Day:** Companies pitch to 500+ top-tier investors.
- **Investor Tracking:** Managing the surge of interest via the YC Dashboard.
- **Handover to Alumni:** Transitioning from "Active Batch" to "Lifelong Alumni" status.

---

## Operating Model (The YC Rules)
- **Build Product & Talk to Users:** If you aren't doing one of these two things, you are wasting time.
- **Launch Early:** Don't wait for perfection. Launch and iterate based on real feedback.
- **Do Things That Don't Scale:** Manually recruit your first 100 users.
- **Growth Focus:** We look for 5-7% week-over-week growth in the core metric.

---

## Technical Onboarding (Founder Stack)
- **CRM/Tracking:** Setup of internal metrics tracking.
- **Perks Activation:** AWS/GCP credits, Stripe Atlas, and software discounts.
- **Bookface Profile:** Ensuring the company is discoverable by the alumni network.

---

## Risk & Escalation
- **Stagnation:** If growth is flat for 3 weeks, immediate partner intervention is required.
- **Co-founder Conflict:** Escalated to partners for mediation or restructuring.
- **Burn Rate:** If "Default Alive" status is threatened without a path to funding.

---

## Done Evidence by Milestone
- **Kickoff:** SAFE signed + Bookface login.
- **Mid-Batch:** 5 consecutive weeks of growth logged.
- **Demo Day:** Final deck uploaded + Handover pack received.
