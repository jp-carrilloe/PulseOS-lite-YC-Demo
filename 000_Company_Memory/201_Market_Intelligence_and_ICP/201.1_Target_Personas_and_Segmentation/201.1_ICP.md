# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Persona Definitions — Y Combinator

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-04
**Author/Editor:** 201_Market_Intel_Agent
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose
The single source of truth for Y Combinator's target audience. All recruitment logic, application evaluation, and founder support must be anchored to the definitions below.

## Executive Summary
- **Priority ICP (Tier 1):** Early-stage technical founders and builders ("The Garage Phase").
- **Secondary ICP (Tier 2):** Successful YC Alumni reaching growth stage (Series B+).
- **Industry Focus:** Industry-agnostic; strong emphasis on software, AI, hardware, biotech, and fintech.
- **Company Stage:** Pre-incorporation to Seed.

---

## Tier 1: Primary ICP — The Formidable Builder

> Technical founders with high obsession, rapid execution speed, and a focus on solving a real problem.

### Who They Are
- **Title:** Founder, CEO, CTO, Lead Engineer.
- **Stage:** Pre-seed / Seed.
- **Company Size:** 1–10 employees.
- **Revenue Range:** $0 (Pre-revenue) to $1M ARR.
- **Geography:** Global (Remote-friendly but focused on the SF/Batch experience).
- **Key Decision Maker:** The Founders.
- **Product / Sales Status:** Minimum Viable Product (MVP) or just a prototype/idea.

### Situation Profile
- Building a product in a high-growth or underserved market.
- Looking for initial capital to quit their jobs or scale their MVP.
- Seeking a network of elite peers and mentors.
- Ready to move to San Francisco for 3 months of intensive focus.
- Obsessed with their users and building product.

### Core Pain Points
- **Lack of Capital:** Difficulty raising seed money without a strong network or traction.
- **Isolation:** Building alone is hard; lack of high-quality peers to bounce ideas off.
- **Speed:** Not knowing how to iterate fast enough or focus on the right metrics.
- **Recruitment:** Hard to hire the first 5 world-class engineers without a brand like YC.

### Why Y Combinator Wins
- **Brand Equity:** YC is the most prestigious name in startups; opens every door.
- **Alumni Network:** Access to 9,000+ founders who have "been there, done that."
- **Standard Deal:** $500k provides immediate runway and removes fundraising stress.
- **Demo Day:** Instant access to the world's top investors in a single day.

### Buying Triggers (Intent Signals)
- Founding team leaving a "Big Tech" company (Google, Meta, OpenAI).
- Recent launch on Product Hunt or Hacker News with high engagement.
- Domain experts starting a company in their field of expertise.
- Serial founders starting their next venture.
- Open-source projects gaining significant GitHub stars.

### Anti-Signals (Disqualifiers)
- Founders focused more on "playing house" (hiring, office space) than building.
- Lack of technical ability within the founding team (for tech startups).
- Unwillingness to listen to feedback or iterate on the idea.
- Focus on slow-growth or non-scalable business models (lifestyle businesses).

---

## Tier 2: Secondary ICPs — Growth Alumni

> Successful YC companies that have reached significant product-market fit and are scaling rapidly.

### Persona A: The Breakout Alum
- **Stage:** Series B+.
- **Company Size:** 50–500 employees.
- **Core Pain:** Managing rapid growth, scaling culture, and raising massive rounds.
- **Why YC Wins:** YC Continuity provides pro-rata capital and growth-stage mentorship.
- **Best Channel:** Internal alumni relations and Bookface data.

---

## Market Segmentation

### Tier 1 Segment Profile
- **Industry:** All Tech (AI, SaaS, Fintech, Crypto, Biotech).
- **Company Size:** 1–5 founders/early employees.
- **Funding Stage:** Pre-seed / Seed.
- **Tech Stack:** High-velocity stacks (Next.js, Python, Rust, etc.).

---

## Exclusion Criteria (Anti-ICP)
- Consultants or agencies (service businesses).
- Companies with no intent to scale or reach venture-scale outcomes.
- "Idea-only" teams with no ability to build the product themselves.
- Founders with a history of unethical behavior or poor reputation in the community.
