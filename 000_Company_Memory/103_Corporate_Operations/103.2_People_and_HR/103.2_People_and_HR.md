## Purpose
Defines how Y Combinator hires and manages the elite team that supports the world's best founders. This section covers the unique partner-led structure and the recruitment of staff to build YC's proprietary software.

---

## 1. Hiring Framework (The YC Way)

### Key Roles
- **Group Partners:** Full-time partners who lead batches and manage founder relationships. Must be successful former founders.
- **Visiting Partners:** Part-time or temporary partners, often alumni founders, who provide specialized expertise during a batch.
- **Software Engineers:** Build and maintain Bookface, Hacker News, and internal dashboard tools.
- **Admissions & Ops:** Manage the massive logistics of the application cycle and Demo Day.

### Sourcing Channels
- **The Alumni Network:** Priority channel for hiring partners and staff.
- **Work at a Startup:** YC's own platform for hiring technical talent.
- **Hacker News:** Primary channel for software engineering roles.

---

## 2. Partner vs. Staff Decision Framework

| Factor | Group Partner | Staff (Eng/Ops) |
|---|---|---|
| Core Role | Leading Batches / Mentorship | Building Tools / Managing Ops |
| Background | Successful Exit / Founder | High-performance Specialist |
| Compensation | Salary + Significant Carry | Salary + Bonus / Equity |
| Engagement | Full-time / Long-term | Full-time |

---

## 3. Onboarding Checklist (The "Batch Ready" Onboarding)

### Pre-Start
- [ ] Partnership agreement / Employment contract signed.
- [ ] Bookface and Hacker News admin credentials prepared.
- [ ] Access to the "Partner Library" (internal playbooks) provisioned.

### Week 1
- [ ] History of YC and "The Strategic Spine" briefing.
- [ ] Shadowing of active office hours or application screening.
- [ ] Introduction to the current batch's "Metric that Matters."

---

## 4. Performance Management

### Review Cadence
- **Batch-based:** Partners are reviewed based on the success and satisfaction of the founders in their group.
- **Annual:** Salary and carry review based on fund performance and long-term impact.

### Success Metrics
- **Founder NPS:** How helpful was the partner to the founders?
- **Company Outcomes:** Survival rates and valuation growth of batch companies.
- **Contribution:** Development of internal knowledge, SOPs, or software features.

---

## 5. Compensation Philosophy
- **Partners:** Competitive base salary with significant participation in the **Carry** of the YC funds (Wealth creation tied to founder success).
- **Staff:** Market-rate salary for top-tier SF talent, plus bonuses and participation in YC's growth.
