## 2. Key SOP: The 10-Minute Interview

**Purpose:** Rapidly evaluate founder quality and market potential in a high-pressure, high-signal format.
**Trigger:** Founders are invited to interview based on application score.
**Steps:**
1. **Pre-read:** Partners spend 2 minutes reviewing the application and notes.
2. **Rapid Q&A:** Partners ask direct questions about growth, technical ability, and product-market fit.
3. **Decision:** Partners vote immediately after the interview (Yes/No/Maybe).
4. **Notification:** Founders are called (Yes) or emailed (No) the same evening.

| 1:1 Office Hours | On-demand | 20 min | Partners | Deep-dive strategic advice |
| Tuesday Dinners | Weekly | 2-3 hrs | Events Lead | Community building and guest speakers |
| Partner Meeting | Weekly | 3 hrs | Garry Tan | Fund strategy and batch health |
| Demo Day | Biannual | Full Day | Partners | Fundraising launch for all startups |

---

## 4. Internal Communication Standards

| Channel | Purpose | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| Slack (#general) | Internal team coordination | Same business day |
| Bookface | Founder-to-founder / Founder-to-partner comms | High priority |
| Hacker News | Public signal and community engagement | Monitoring |
| Email | Formal / Legal / Application support | Within 24 hours |

**Rules:**
- **Advice is not an order:** Partners give advice; founders make decisions.
- **Directness:** We value unvarnished truth over politeness.
- **Privacy:** Bookface discussions are private to the YC community.

---

## 5. Project Management Framework (The Batch Engine)

**Tool:** Custom Internal Dashboard (The YC Dashboard)

**Structure:**
- **Milestones:** Focus on "The Metric that Matters" (usually revenue or usage growth).
- **Tracking:** Founders update their metrics weekly for visibility.
- **Blockers:** Flagged during Group Office Hours for immediate partner/peer resolution.
