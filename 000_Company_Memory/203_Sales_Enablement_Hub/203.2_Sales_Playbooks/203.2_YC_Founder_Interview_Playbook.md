# YC Founder Interview Playbook

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-04
**Author/Editor:** @ARK
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose
A tactical guide for the high-intensity 10-minute YC interview. This is our primary "sales" motion where we filter for the world's most formidable founders. The goal is to move fast, test for depth of understanding, and identify "earnestness" and "velocity."

---

## The 10-Minute Framework

### Phase 1: Problem Depth (0-3 Minutes)
- **Objective:** Determine if the founders truly understand the problem they are solving.
- **Key Questions:**
    - What are you building? (Looking for clarity and brevity).
    - Who needs this most right now?
    - How do you know they need it? (Test for user conversations).
    - What do you understand about this problem that others don't? (The Insight).

### Phase 2: Technical & Execution Velocity (3-7 Minutes)
- **Objective:** Test the speed at which the team moves and their technical competence.
- **Key Questions:**
    - When did you start working on this?
    - What have you built since then?
    - How many users/customers do you have today?
    - (For technical founders): Why did you choose this tech stack? What's the hardest part of the build?

### Phase 3: Market & Ambition (7-10 Minutes)
- **Objective:** Determine if this can become a giant company (Unicorn potential).
- **Key Questions:**
    - How big can this get?
    - Why hasn't this been done before?
    - Why are you the right team to win this market?
    - What's the next thing you're going to build?

---

## The "Sales" Decision (The Close)
- **Filter 1: Founder Quality.** Are they smart, fast, determined, and earnest?
- **Filter 2: Rate of Learning.** How fast have they iterated since they started?
- **Filter 3: Market Insight.** Do they have a non-obvious insight into a growing market?

**The Result:**
- **Yes:** "We're in. We'll send you the $500k standard deal docs tonight."
- **No:** "Not right now. Here is the direct feedback on why we're passing."

---

## Internal Objection Handling (Partner Debrief)
- "The market is too small." -> *Counter:* "Airbnb looked small. Is the founder formidable enough to expand it?"
- "The team is too early." -> *Counter:* "Velocity is high. They built the MVP in 2 weeks. Fund the speed."
- "There is too much competition." -> *Counter:* "What's their non-obvious insight? Do they move faster than the incumbents?"
