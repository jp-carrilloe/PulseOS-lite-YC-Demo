## Purpose
Strategic outreach sequence designed to identify and recruit "Formidable Builders" from Big Tech, AI labs, and academia. The focus is on challenging the status quo and offering the "Standard Deal" as a path to ownership and impact.

---

## Sequence: The "Ownership" Recruitment (AI/Deep Tech)

### Channel: LinkedIn + Email
**Target Persona:** Senior Engineers / Researchers at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, etc.

- **Day 1: The Intellectual Interrupt (Email/LI DM)**
    - **Hook:** "You're building the future at {{company}}, but you don't own it."
    - **Message:** "I've been following your work on {{project/topic}}. Builders like you should be owners. YC turns engineers into formidable founders. We're looking for the next generation of AI giants for the upcoming batch."
    - **CTA:** Low friction. "Open to a 10-minute chat on how YC can fund your own thing?"

- **Day 3: The Proof of Velocity (Email)**
    - **Hook:** "What could you build in 11 weeks?"
    - **Value:** Reference a successful YC alum from their background (e.g., "Like {{Alumni_Name}} who left Google to build {{YC_Company}}").
    - **Message:** "The real value of YC isn't the $500k; it's the distribution engine and the pattern recognition of 4,000+ founders. We help you move 10x faster."

- **Day 7: The Distribution Signal (LinkedIn)**
    - **Tactic:** Like/Comment on their recent technical post + short DM.
    - **Message:** "Great insight on {{topic}}. This is the kind of depth we look for in founders. If you ever think about spinning this out into a company, let's talk. We move fast."

- **Day 14: The Batch Deadline (Email)**
    - **Hook:** "Batch applications closing."
    - **Message:** "We're finalizing the shortlist for the next batch. Teams like yours usually thrive here. If you're ready to make something people want, the application is here. If you want to skip the line and chat first, let me know."

---

## Personalization Variables
- `{{first_name}}` — Founder's first name.
- `{{company}}` — Current employer (e.g., OpenAI, Meta).
- `{{project/topic}}` — Specific technical interest or project they've worked on.
- `{{Alumni_Name}}` — Relevant YC alum for social proof.
- `{{YC_Company}}` — Company built by that alum.
