## Purpose
A framework for managing Y Combinator's strategic alliances with technology providers and service firms. These partnerships provide exclusive benefits (YC Perks) to batch companies, reducing their burn and accelerating their technical execution.

## Program Structure
- **Value Proposition to Partners:** Early access to the world's highest-potential startups. Partners capture future market leaders at the "day zero" stage.
- **Engagement Model:** Partnership agreements involving cloud credits, discounted software, and dedicated support channels for YC founders.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Non-dilutive value. Partners provide free or heavily discounted services in exchange for being the "default" choice for YC companies.

## 3. Partner Onboarding & Governance
1. **Initial Audit:** Verify the partner's value-add to early-stage technical founders.
2. **Standardization:** Partner provides a specific "YC Deal" link or code accessible via Bookface.
3. **Execution:** Joint "Partner Office Hours" scheduled during the batch to help founders integrate the tools.

## Required Assets
- **YC Perks Directory (Bookface):** The centralized list of all active deals.
- **Partner Office Hours Schedule:** Coordinated via the Admissions/Ops team.
