## Purpose
Documentation of Y Combinator's investment terms, fee structures, and commercial architecture. For YC, "pricing" refers to the equity cost of the investment and the value provided.

## Pricing Philosophy
YC uses a standardized, non-negotiable deal for every company. This philosophy prioritizes fairness, speed, and transparency, ensuring all founders in a batch are on equal footing.

## The Standard Deal

### Tier 1: The YC Batch Investment
- **Investment Amount:** $500,000
- **Equity Cost:** 7% (Post-money SAFE) + MFN Rights on $375k.
- **What's Included:**
    - $125k for 7% equity.
    - $375k on an uncapped SAFE with a "Most Favored Nation" (MFN) clause.
    - Full participation in the 11-week batch.
    - Access to Bookface and the YC alumni network for life.
    - Demo Day participation.
- **Target Customer:** Early-stage startups (Pre-seed/Seed).

### Tier 2: YC Continuity (Growth)
- **Investment Amount:** Varies (typically millions).
- **Equity Cost:** Negotiated based on market rates (Series B+).
- **What's Included:**
    - Growth stage capital.
    - Participation in Series B/C rounds.
- **Target Customer:** Successful YC Alumni.
