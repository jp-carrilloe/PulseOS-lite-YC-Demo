## 201.1. Purpose / Objective
This document defines the high-level brand identity, voice guidelines, and visual principles for **Y Combinator**. It ensures that all communications and assets remain aligned with the core strategic identity of the business: a launchpad for formidable founders.

## 201.2. Executive Summary
*   **Brand Core:** Y Combinator is defined as **The World's Premier Startup Accelerator and Community**.
*   **The Tone:** Focus on **Directness** and **Founder-Centricity**.
*   **Visual Philosophy:** Minimalist, functional, and iconic (Orange & White).

---

## 🗣️ Brand Voice

### 102. The Tone
*   **Direct & Unvarnished:** We speak the truth, even when it's hard. No fluff, no corporate jargon.
*   **Founder-Centric:** Every piece of advice and communication is aimed at helping the founder, not the investor or the institution.
*   **High-Signal:** Every word should provide value. We prioritize brevity and clarity over elaborate prose.
*   **Intellectual & Pragmatic:** We value logic, data, and what actually works in the real world.

### 201. The Vocabulary
*   ✅ **Preferred Terms:** "Make something people want", "Formidable", "Default Alive", "High signal", "Founder-led", "The Batch", "Demo Day".
*   ❌ **Avoided Terms:** "Synergy", "Thought leadership", "Disruptive" (when used as fluff), "Holistic", "Value-add" (without specific context).

---

## 🖼️ Visual Principles (Guidelines for Designers)

*   **Design Philosophy:** Clean, minimalist, and functional. The content (the founders and their companies) is the hero, not the design.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary:** YC Orange (#FF6600) — Used for the logo and primary calls to action.
    *   **Background:** White (#FFFFFF) — Used for clarity and focus.
    *   **Text:** Charcoal/Black (#222222) — For high readability.
*   **Typography:** Clean sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Helvetica, or system defaults) that prioritize legibility across all devices.

---

## ✍️ Writing Style (The "Scanner" Rule)
YC content should be easily scannable and packed with information.
*   **Structure:** Use short paragraphs and clear headings.
*   **Emphasis:** Use bolding for key takeaways and actionable advice.
*   **Verb Choice:** Use active, direct verbs. "Build product," "Talk to users," "Apply now."
