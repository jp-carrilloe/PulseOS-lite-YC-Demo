## Purpose
Defines the "North Star" goals, founding principles, and core values that guide **Y Combinator's** culture and the autonomous logic of its Agent Economy.

## Mission Statement
YC turns builders into formidable founders.

## Vision Statement
To be the world's premier launchpad for companies that change the world, providing the capital, community, and guidance to help founders make something people want.

## Core Motto
"Make something people want."

## Core Values
- **Founder-Centricity:** Founders are the lifeblood of YC. We provide advice, not orders, and prioritize the founder's vision.
- **High Intensity:** The 11-week batch is a sprint designed to force rapid iteration and focus.
- **Raw Productivity:** Cut through the noise and focus on the two things that matter: talking to users and building product.
- **Scale through Software:** Use software to scale the amount of help we can give to the maximum number of founders.
- **Lifelong Community:** Being part of YC is a lifelong commitment to a network of elite peers.

## Founding Principles
- **Launch Early:** Don't wait for perfection. Launch, get feedback, and iterate.
- **Default Alive:** Founders should aim to reach profitability as quickly as possible to control their own destiny.
- **Directness:** We give direct, unvarnished feedback because that's what founders need to hear.
- **Growth Focus:** If you're not growing, you're not learning enough from your users.
