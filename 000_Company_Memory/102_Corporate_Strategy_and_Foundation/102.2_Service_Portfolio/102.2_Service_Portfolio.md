## Portfolio Overview

Y Combinator provides a launchpad for startups through a structured 11-week batch program, significant seed capital, and a lifelong network of founders. We deliver the "standard deal" of $500k to every company we accept, along with intensive mentorship and software tools to help them grow.

---

## Part A: Products (Investments & Software)

### Product 1: The YC Standard Deal (Investment)

- **Type:** Seed Investment / SAFE
- **Description:** A total investment of $500,000 into every company accepted into the program.
- **Core Value Proposition:** Provides immediate runway and a standardized, founder-friendly investment structure.
- **Target Customer:** Early-stage founders (pre-seed to seed).
- **Key Features / Capabilities:**
  - $125,000 on a post-money SAFE in return for 7% of the company.
  - $375,000 on an uncapped SAFE with a Most Favored Nation (MFN) clause.
- **Pricing Model:** Equity-based (7% + MFN).
- **Delivery / Availability:** biannual batches (Winter and Summer).
- **Current Status:** Live

### Product 2: Bookface (Founder Hub)

- **Type:** Internal SaaS / Community Platform
- **Description:** A private social network and knowledge base for the 9,000+ YC founders.
- **Core Value Proposition:** Instant access to the collective knowledge and connections of the YC community.
- **Target Customer:** Current YC founders and alumni.
- **Key Features / Capabilities:**
  - Q&A forum for founder-specific problems.
  - Directory of all YC founders and companies.
  - Exclusive deals and discounts on software (e.g., AWS, Stripe, GCP credits).
- **Pricing Model:** Included in the YC program (No additional cost).
- **Delivery / Availability:** Web and Mobile App.
- **Current Status:** Live

## Part B: Services (Mentorship & Programs)

### Service 1: The YC Batch Program

- **Type:** 11-week Accelerator
- **Description:** An intensive program where founders focus exclusively on building their product and talking to users.
- **Core Value Proposition:** Accelerates a company's progress to be in "dramatically better shape" within 3 months.
- **Target Customer:** Early-stage startups.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Weekly group office hours with YC partners.
  - One-on-one office hours for deep dives.
  - Weekly dinners with guest speakers (successful founders, investors).
  - Demo Day: The culmination where founders pitch to the world's top investors.
- **Engagement Model:** 11-week intensive sprint.
- **Pricing Model:** Part of the equity deal.
- **Current Status:** Active

### Service 2: YC Continuity

- **Type:** Growth Stage Support
- **Description:** A fund specifically for growth-stage YC companies (Series B and beyond).
- **Core Value Proposition:** Ongoing capital and support as companies scale to thousands of employees.
- **Target Customer:** Successful YC alumni.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Pro-rata investment in later rounds.
  - Participation in Series B and C rounds.
  - Growth-stage specific mentorship and programs.
- **Engagement Model:** Long-term partnership.
- **Pricing Model:** Venture capital investment.
- **Current Status:** Active

## Part C: How Products and Services Work Together

### Combined Offering Architecture

Founders enter YC through the **Standard Deal** (Investment Product) and the **Batch Program** (Service). During the batch, they use **Bookface** (Software Product) to scale their learning and network. Post-batch, they remain active in the **YC Community** and may receive further investment from **YC Continuity**.

### Entry Points

| Entry Point | Starts With | Typical Upsell Path |
|---|---|---|
| Main Batch | Standard Deal + Batch | → Demo Day → Seed/Series A → YC Continuity |
| Startup School | Online Course (Free) | → Apply to YC Batch |
| Work at a Startup | Hiring Platform | → Join YC company → Become a founder → Apply to YC |
