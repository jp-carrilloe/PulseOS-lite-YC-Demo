## Overview
This document outlines the financial modeling logic for Y Combinator's fund management and batch investment cycle. YC's model is unique, focusing on high-volume, standardized seed investments and long-term equity appreciation.

### Key Variables to Track:
- **Batch Size:** Number of companies accepted per batch (Winter/Summer).
- **Investment Outflow:** $500,000 per company (Total = Batch Size * $500k).
- **Equity Stake:** 7% post-money SAFE + MFN rights.
- **OpEx (Management Fees):** Staff salaries, Bookface maintenance, Demo Day production, Tuesday Dinners.
- **Carry:** The percentage of profits returned to the partners after LPs are paid back.
- **Graduation Rate:** The percentage of batch companies that successfully raise a Seed or Series A round.

---

## Financial Architecture

### 1. Fund Cycle
YC raises funds from Limited Partners (LPs) to cover multiple batches and growth-stage investments (Continuity).
- **Capital Calls:** Triggered biannually to fund the next batch.
- **Management Fee:** Typically 2% per year to cover operations.

### 2. Unit Economics (The "Deal" Model)
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Cost of marketing, application screening, and interviews per accepted company.
- **Investment Cost:** $500,000.
- **Target Outcome:** Participation in companies that reach $1B+ valuations (unicorns), where the 7% stake covers the cost of the entire batch and more.

### 3. Runway & Burn
- **Internal Burn:** Monthly cost of running the YC organization (Staff, Software, Facilities).
- **Fund Runway:** Calculated based on the number of batches and follow-on reserves remaining in the current fund.
