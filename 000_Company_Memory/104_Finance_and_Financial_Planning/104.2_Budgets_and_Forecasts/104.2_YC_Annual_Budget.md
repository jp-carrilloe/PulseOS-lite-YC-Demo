## Overview
This document manages the allocation of management fees and fund capital across Y Combinator's operational departments. It ensures that the "Batch Engine" remains fully funded and that follow-on reserves are projected accurately.

---

## 1. Operational Department Budgets

### Software & Infrastructure (The Digital Campus)
- **Bookface & Internal Tools:** Development and maintenance.
- **Hacker News Ops:** Server costs and moderation staff.
- **AI Integration:** Token usage for automated application screening and founder support tools.

### Events & Batch Experience
- **Tuesday Dinners:** Catering and logistics for 250+ founders weekly during the batch.
- **Demo Day Production:** High-end venue, streaming, and investor coordination.
- **Founder Retreats:** Onboarding and mid-batch offsites.

### People & Partners
- **Partner Compensation:** Base salaries + performance-linked carry.
- ** visiting Partners:** Stipends and expenses.
- **Admissions & Ops:** Staffing for application cycles.

---

## 2. Investment Forecasting

### Batch Capacity Forecasting
- **Winter/Summer Targets:** Projecting batch size based on fund capacity and partner bandwidth.
- **Deal Allocation:** Forecasting the total $500k checks to be issued in the next 12–24 months.

### Follow-on Reserves (Continuity)
- **Pro-rata Projections:** Forecasting the capital required to maintain 7% ownership in breakout companies during their Series A/B rounds.
- **Liquidity Events:** Forecasting potential exits and distributions to LPs.

---

## 3. Hiring Roadmap & Triggers
- **Partner Hiring:** Triggered when the partner-to-founder ratio exceeds 1:10.
- **Engineer Hiring:** Triggered by major feature roadmaps for Bookface or scaling needs for Hacker News.
