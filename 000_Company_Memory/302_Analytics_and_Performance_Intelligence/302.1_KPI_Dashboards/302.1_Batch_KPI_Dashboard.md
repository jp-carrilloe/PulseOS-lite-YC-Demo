**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-04
**Author/Editor:** @ARK
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose
Real-time tracking of the current batch's performance and the long-term health of the Y Combinator portfolio.

---

## 1. The Metric that Matters (Batch Health)
YC focuses on a single "North Star" metric per company, typically revenue or active users.

| Metric | Target | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **WoW Growth** | 5-7% | Week-over-week growth in the core metric. |
| **Launch Rate** | 100% | % of batch companies with a live product by Week 3. |
| **User Conversations** | 10+ / week | Average number of direct user interviews per founder. |
| **Partner Office Hours** | 100% | Attendance at mandatory group office hours. |

---

## 2. Demo Day & Graduation Metrics
Tracking the immediate output of the batch.

| Metric | Target | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Demo Day Readiness** | 100% | % of companies with an approved pitch and deck by Week 10. |
| **Investor Interest** | 20+ leads | Average number of high-intent investor leads per company. |
| **Graduation Rate** | >80% | % of companies that raise a seed round within 6 months. |

---

## 3. Long-Term Portfolio Analytics (The Flywheel)
Tracking the "pattern recognition" success.

| Metric | Category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Unicorn Rate** | Success | % of companies reaching $1B+ valuation. |
| **Batch Survivability** | Health | % of companies "Default Alive" at the 2-year mark. |
| **Follow-on Capital** | Distribution | Total capital raised by alumni companies. |
| **Alumni NPS** | Community | Likelihood of founders to refer their best peers. |

---
